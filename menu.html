Cómo funciona

How the pastry wall works

1

Grab a tray & tongs

They’re waiting by the pastry wall.

2

Pick what looks good

Every bin is labeled — conchas, besos, orejas, marranitos, semitas and more.

3

Bring it to the counter

Add a slice of tres leches, a flan or a juice, pay by card or phone, and you’re set.
