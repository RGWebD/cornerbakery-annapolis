Cómo pedir

How custom cakes work

1

Share your idea

Call (443) 949-0309 or email a photo, theme or color palette to bakerygalleria@icloud.com.

2

Pick flavor & size

Tell us how many guests and what you love — tres leches layers, choco flan, fruit, chocolate. The team will talk you through options and pricing.

3

We bake & decorate

“The staff listened to what I wanted and surpassed our expectation!” — jeffey m.

4

Pick up & celebrate

Pick your cake up at the shop on Forest Drive (the bakery doesn’t deliver) — grab a piñata while you’re here.

Pide tu pastel

Start a cake request

Fill this in and it opens a ready-to-send email to the bakery — attach an inspiration photo if you have one. Prefer to talk? Call (443) 949-0309.

Order timing and pricing depend on the design — the bakery will confirm both with you. Cakes are picked up at the shop.
